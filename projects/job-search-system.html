1# Job-search workflow 2- Find: Match’up, Welcome to the Jungle, company sites 3- Check fit: alternance contract, English-friendly 4- Tailor: Claude drafts from my current CV 5- Review: I read every line before sending 6- Track: Notion → Opportunities, Applications, Tasks

1. The goal

Apply for alternance roles across several sectors without losing track of where I applied, what I sent, and who I need to follow up with.

2. My approach

  1. 01

    Find

    Alternance offers on OMNES Match’up, Welcome to the Jungle and company career sites — business development, operations, retail, hospitality and luxury.

  2. 02

    Check fit

    Is it a real alternance contract? Does the team work in English or welcome English speakers?

  3. 03

    Tailor

    Claude helps me adapt my CV and cover letter to the role, always starting from my current CV. I review everything before sending.

  4. 04

    Track

    Each opportunity and application goes into my Notion command centre, with tasks for follow-ups and replies.

3. What works

  • Finding and shortlisting alternance offers across several sectors
  • Tailored CV and cover-letter drafts based on my current CV
  • One place to track applications, replies and follow-ups

4. Limits & next steps

  • It doesn’t apply for me — every application is reviewed and sent by hand.
  • Next: use the same tracking for the international opportunities I’m exploring in parallel.
